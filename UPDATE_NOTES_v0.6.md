# HFR Encyclopedia v0.6 — North America Globe Prototype

## Added

- New **Interactive Globe** page under World.
- Draggable orthographic globe with limited zoom and polity focus controls.
- Clickable North American political regions with encyclopedia links where articles exist.
- Custom HFR North America GeoJSON political polygons and border layers.
- Real geographic coastline, lake, and river reference data used as the visual base.
- Great Basin Mosaic left deliberately unfilled.
- Dashed unresolved MDE southern frontier around northern Nicaragua.
- Visible Baja topology-review segment rather than silently inventing a final connection.
- Confirmed island ownership markers for PEI, the Magdalen Islands, Anticosti Island, and Isla Guadalupe.
- Scandinavian Greenland/Arctic territory shown in the Vinland color family while administration remains TBD.

## Cartographic rule

Coordinates and named locations are control points, not instructions to draw straight lines. Named rivers, ridgelines, roads, coastlines, lakes, and other geographic features remain the intended border path. Some named-anchor interpolation in this first technical prototype is still approximate and is not new canon.

## No lore retcons

This release primarily implements already-approved map decisions. It does not resolve the MDE southern frontier, internal Great Basin polities, most Caribbean ownership, or the exact Vinland/Greenland Arctic colonial administration.
