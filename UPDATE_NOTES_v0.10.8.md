# HFR Encyclopedia v0.10.8 — Caesar's Legions Article Expansion

Date: 2026-08-27

## Scope

This release is an **article/editorial update only**. The interactive-globe geometry, river network, border geometry, political fills, and interaction behavior remain unchanged from the stable v0.10.6 map baseline.

## Caesar's Legions article

The former compact CL dossier has been replaced by a full national article covering:

- Roman Gulf origins and the long religious-cultural divergence from the Atlantic colonies;
- the formation and historical importance of **Liga Caesarea**;
- more than five centuries of Gulf participation inside the USA without erasing regional identity;
- the Great Indian War from the Gulf perspective, emphasizing federal abandonment narratives rather than duplicating the dedicated event article;
- **Mr C** and the **Miracle on the Bayou** as the core founder-memory of modern CL;
- the postwar political crisis, secession in 1575, and the Legionary Civil War through 1581;
- a preferred **decentralized military-commonwealth** model that preserves genuine civic and regional institutions while giving the legions exceptional political prestige;
- Mr C as national founder, military savior, and near-sacred civic hero without canonizing him as a god or prophet;
- pluralist **Gulf-Roman paganism** as a living American religious tradition rather than a frozen reconstruction of ancient Roman religion;
- broad Christian toleration, including syncretic Gulf Christian communities, while leaving possible restrictions on active proselytization unresolved;
- Gulf Occidental language identity and continuing Atlantic Standard bilingualism in parts of the state;
- regional geography, economy, infrastructure, industry, military strategy, river/Gulf forces, and internal security;
- foreign relations with the USA, MDE, UTA, New Vegas, and NCR;
- the Legionary national myth of restoration and the contradiction that CL is simultaneously restoring an older Gulf identity and creating a new war-born society;
- greatest fear, greatest desire, 1604 strategic position, source-status table, and expanded TBD registry.

## Geography correction

The article now explicitly defers present-day territory to the **Interactive Globe and current project border corrections**. Obsolete prose stating that CL holds southern Alabama or that its present frontier remains broadly TBD has been removed.

## Map safety

No GeoJSON, globe geometry, river data, border data, selection behavior, or globe styling was intentionally modified for v0.10.8.
