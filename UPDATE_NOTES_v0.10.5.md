# HFR Encyclopedia v0.10.5

## Border & Selection Polish

This patch deliberately changes **map rendering only**. No political border geometry or polity ownership was edited.

### Changes
- All political borders render black.
- River-frontier overlays render black above the blue hydrology layer, so the international boundary takes visual precedence wherever the two coincide.
- Loose, provisional, and review frontiers remain distinguishable by dash pattern rather than colored strokes.
- Clicking a polity now brightens the selected fill and gently mutes other mapped polities.
- The selected polity's complete perimeter is redrawn above rivers and political-border layers with a thicker black outline, making inland borders as legible as coastlines.
- Current release/version labels updated to v0.10.5.

### Explicit non-changes
- No border coordinates changed.
- No political fills or ownership changed.
- No river hierarchy or river geometry changed.
- No articles or lore changed.
