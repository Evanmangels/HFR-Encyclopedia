## Great Indian War continuity

The Iroquois theater now explicitly includes sustained battles through urbanized Columbea and Nova Caeserea, the coastal drive that isolates Boralea, and the western attempt to connect through the Great Lakes/Chicago-region corridor with the UTA. The campaign outline follows current project direction, later Master Doc V3, and the detailed operational material in Rome (With Colonialism). Exact battle names, commanders, city lines, and retreat chronology remain TBD.
