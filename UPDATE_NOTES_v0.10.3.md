# HFR Encyclopedia v0.10.3 — Iroquois & River-System Rebuild

## Scope

Focused globe cartography update. No new lore.

## USA–Iroquois

- Converted the approved USA–Iroquois border to shared topology.
- The visible line now partitions both political fills, including the Great Lakes water frontier.

## Rivers

- Replaces the Natural Earth 1:10m North America supplement display with the simpler 1:50m base river network.
- Filters to named North American river features.
- All features belonging to the same normalized river name inherit one visual importance class, eliminating mid-course visibility/thickness jumps caused by segment-level ranks.
- Major rivers are visible at all zooms; medium rivers appear at regional zoom; minor rivers appear only at close zoom.
- Dense bundled minor hydrology is suppressed as a normal fallback; bundled major and medium layers remain available offline.
