# HFR Encyclopedia v0.10.7 — United States Article Expansion

Date: 2026-08-27

## Scope

This release is an **article/editorial update only**. The interactive-globe geometry, river network, border geometry, political fills, and interaction behavior from v0.10.6 are unchanged.

## United States article

The former compact USA dossier has been replaced by a full national article covering:

- Roman-colonial origins and the federation sequence beginning in 670 ATL;
- the distinct Gulf development of Liga Caesarea;
- the WIP **Plains Mounted Confederation** as the neutral encyclopedia term for the early Sioux-linked mounted interior power;
- Cahokia's wider historical role and the later **Razing of Cahokia**, including its importance to the end of the surviving Mound political world;
- an explicit note that centuries of American continental expansion included many wars that have not yet been individually named or canonized;
- a national-history summary of the **First Great Indian War**, focused on the events and consequences most important to the USA rather than duplicating the dedicated war article;
- a tighter national-history summary of the **Legionary Civil War** and the permanent split between the USA and Caesar's Legions;
- the postwar constitutional trajectory: surviving republican institutions, normalized emergency authority, centralist electoral dominance after Gulf secession, and a soft-canon drift toward a proto-imperial republic;
- religion, Atlantic Standard, regional identity, economy, infrastructure, armed forces, internal security, society, and foreign relations;
- a new **Former Territories and Territorial Claims** subsection;
- **wounded restorationism** as the defining political mood of the USA in 1604 ATL;
- a refreshed canon/source-status table and expanded open-question registry.

## Naming correction applied

Historic state spellings now follow the current linguistic distinction in the USA article:

- Atlantic Standard-majority: **Columbea, Nova Caesarea, Carolinea, Augustea**;
- Gulf Occidental / pagan: **Texia, Missia, Appalachia, Floria**.

## Map safety

No GeoJSON or globe geometry was modified for v0.10.7. Present borders in the USA article explicitly defer to the current Interactive Globe where older prose summaries conflict with newer cartographic work.
