# HFR Encyclopedia v0.8 — Precision Globe Pass

**Date:** 2026-08-21

v0.8 is a focused interactive-globe correction release. No unrelated setting lore was added.

## Interaction fixes
- Full political and land geometry now stays mounted while dragging.
- Only rivers/lakes switch to lightweight interaction geometry, eliminating the drag-time ocean-color artifact while keeping hydrology in motion.
- Maximum zoom is increased again, with a closer New Vegas focus.

## Cartographic fixes
- Great Basin Mosaic receives a muted beige-gray fill while remaining explicitly fragmented internally.
- CL borders are substantially densified and fitted to the specified river and ridge corridors.
- Vinland–UTA, Vinland–アラスカ, and UTA–アラスカ boundaries are substantially densified and fitted to the stated hydrology and control corridors.
- Lake Erie and Lake Superior water boundaries are routed through the lakes.
- The USA–Vinland St. Lawrence boundary is kept in the river/gulf rather than crossing southeastern Quebec toward Anticosti.
- Olympic/Pacific Northwest ownership, Greenland, Iceland, the lower Mackenzie, Cuba exclusion, and lower Mississippi/Delta ownership are retained/corrected according to current project decisions.

## Navigation
- Interactive Globe is now a top-level navigation tab.

## Canon note
All generated intermediate geometry is implementation detail. User-supplied control points and explicitly stated geographic features remain the authoritative border specification.
