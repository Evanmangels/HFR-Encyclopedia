## Overview

**1604 ATL** is the fixed present year of How Few Remain. The world has reached a broad technological and industrial level comparable to **OTL c. 1910**, but through a radically different civilizational history. Rome lasted longer, reached the Americas, and left successor systems in both hemispheres. Indigenous powers survived at great-power scale. Persia, Byzantium, the [Golden Horde](../polities/golden-horde.md), [Novgorod](../polities/novgorod.md), Hijez, and multiple Roman-successor states remain active competitors.

The present is best described as a **multipolar armed peace**. Recent wars have redrawn both Europe and North America without resolving the grievances that caused them.

## Technology and state capacity

Major powers possess:

- mature industrial production;
- modern rifles and machine guns;
- quick-firing and heavy artillery;
- rail-borne mobilization and supply;
- telegraphic command and commercial networks;
- industrial fleets, ports, coaling and maintenance systems;
- censuses, customs services, police systems, schools and modern tax bureaucracies.

Industrialization does not erase older identities. Roman successor states, Orthodox commercial empires, steppe powers, Indigenous confederacies and Islamic restoration states use modern administration to project older legitimacy systems.

### Aviation lag

Aircraft exist, but aviation remains in a prototype phase. It is not yet a normal foundation of military reconnaissance, tactical support, strategic bombing or mass commercial transport. Railways, rivers, cavalry, observation systems, telegraph lines and human scouting therefore remain strategically central.

## North America

North America is a multipolar industrial continent.

| Power | Position in 1604 ATL |
|---|---|
| [United States](../polities/united-states.md) | Wounded but major Atlantic federation, de facto east of the Mississippi. |
| [Caesar's Legions](../polities/caesars-legions.md) | Independent pagan Gulf/interior Roman-successor state west and south of the Mississippi, north of the Rio Grande, with part of southern Alabama. |
| [Mexica Double Eagle Empire](../polities/mexica-double-eagle-empire.md) | Great power of the south and southwest, controlling the Rio Grande/New Mexico corridor. |
| [United Tribes of America](../polities/united-tribes-of-america.md) | Major plains and interior confederational power, holding gains to roughly Iowa–Nebraska. |
| [Iroquois Confederacy](../polities/iroquois-confederacy.md) | Major northeastern power in the Lake Ontario–St. Lawrence interior. |
| [NCR](../polities/new-california-republic.md) | Independent California, separated from the USA after the Great Indian War. |
| [New Vegas](../polities/new-vegas.md) | Neutral city-state and Mojave rail hinge. |
| [Vinland](../polities/vinland.md) | Northern Atlantic power tied to Scandinavia and allied with the Iroquois. |

The continent is still living inside the consequences of the [First Great Indian War](../history/events/first-great-indian-war.md) and [Legionary Civil War](../history/events/legionary-civil-war.md).

## Western and central Europe

The western system is dominated by the unresolved rivalry between [Francia](../polities/francia.md) and the [Holy Roman Empire](../polities/holy-roman-empire.md).

- The HRE controls Germany, Switzerland, Austria, Bohemia, northern Italy to Rome, and most former Danelaw lands.
- Francia retains Gaul, a northern Hispania belt, western England and Wales.
- The Celtic Union holds Ireland, Scotland and North Umbria.
- The [Franco-Roman War](../history/events/franco-roman-war.md) has made the HRE the leading continental power and Francia a wounded revisionist state.
- Al-Andalus remains a major western Islamic and maritime power in southern Iberia and on the Moroccan coast.

Exact alliance blocs and final colonial holdings remain **TBD**.

## Northern and eastern Europe

[Novgorod](../polities/novgorod.md) controls the unified Rus core to the Urals, Finland, Estonia, and northern/eastern Livonia. It rose through tributary survival under the [Golden Horde](../polities/golden-horde.md), victory over Muscovy, and the [Novgorod–Scandinavian Wars](../history/events/novgorod-scandinavian-wars.md).

[Poland–Lithuania](../polities/poland-lithuania.md) is a vast decentralized commonwealth between the HRE, Novgorod, and the steppe. Its intervention in the 1550s war gives it Riga, Curonia, and southern Livonia. Victory reinforces its decentralized system and excessive confidence in cavalry-centered warfare.

[Scandinavia](../polities/scandinavia.md) still unites Denmark, Sweden, and Norway. It loses Finland and the eastern Baltic but responds by centralizing military command, taxation, conscription, and foreign policy. It remains a major maritime state through the Øresund, Iceland, Greenland, and [Vinland](../polities/vinland.md).

The [Golden Horde](../polities/golden-horde.md) remains a durable non-Islamic steppe great power across much of the open Ukrainian steppe, Donbas, Alash, and connected corridors. Its confrontation with Novgorod is one of the world's most dangerous unresolved frontiers.

## Eastern Mediterranean and Near East

[Byzantium](../polities/byzantium.md) controls the Bosporus and Dardanelles, most of Anatolia, and the southern Balkans. Its sovereignty over the Straits is hard canon; the exact passage, dues and neutrality regime is **TBD**.

[Persia](../polities/persia.md) remains a major Iranian plateau state but has fallen behind the leading western industrial powers. Its relationship with Hijez and the exact Mesopotamian frontier are **TBD**.

The [Hijez Sultanate](../polities/hijez-sultanate.md) now includes Egypt and the former Abbasid polity. This current project correction supersedes older V3 present-day passages that treated Egypt and the Abbasids as independent rivals.

## Colonial and global systems

European and Islamic imperialism exists through:

- ports and islands;
- coastal colonies;
- concessions and protectorates;
- chartered trade and industrial extraction;
- strategic corridors and naval stations.

Exact ownership across Africa, South America, Southeast Asia, India, Australia and the Pacific remains status-sensitive and should not be presented as fully locked. Japan is a major Pacific imperial actor, but Asia remains under active development.

## Political atmosphere

An educated observer in 1604 would likely see a world of **strained greatness**:

- every major state is heavily armed;
- recent wars have failed to settle core rivalries;
- rail and telegraph systems make mobilization faster and crises harder to contain;
- public legitimacy is tied to civilizational grievance, religion, imperial succession and historical restoration;
- the exact spark of a general Great War remains unknown.

See [Geopolitical Tensions in 1604](geopolitical-tensions.md) for the principal fault lines.

## Canon cautions

- The present year is exactly **1604 ATL**.
- Egypt and the Abbasids are not independent in 1604; they are within Hijez.
- Denmark is not independent; it remains within centralized Scandinavia.
- Novgorod does not control every Baltic territory: Poland–Lithuania holds Riga, Curonia, and southern Livonia.
- The world is industrial-modern, not alt-medieval.
- Aviation is lagging relative to the broader c. 1910-equivalent baseline.
- Colonial maps outside the best-developed regions remain incomplete.
