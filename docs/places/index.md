# Places

This section will contain articles for cities, provinces, geographic regions, seas, islands, frontiers, ports, transportation corridors, and major infrastructure.

## Article rules

1. Identify the controlling polity and the article date.
2. Record alternate names and the languages in which they are used.
3. Separate established population figures from estimates.
4. Describe transportation and trade connections geographically, not just politically.
5. Flag any border or map conflict before treating it as resolved.

Use the [city template](../templates/city-template.md) for new city articles.
