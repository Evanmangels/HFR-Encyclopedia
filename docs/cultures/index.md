# Peoples & Cultures

Culture articles should distinguish among political citizenship, ethnicity, language, religion, regional identity, and social class rather than treating them as interchangeable.

## Suggested article structure

- Identity and self-designation
- Geographic distribution
- Languages and dialects
- Religion and institutions
- Social organization
- Material culture
- Education and literacy
- Historical development
- Internal regional or class differences
- External stereotypes and political narratives
- Naming conventions
