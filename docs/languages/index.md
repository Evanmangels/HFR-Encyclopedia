# Languages

The language section covers languages, scripts, spelling standards, dialects, pronunciation, grammar, naming practices, titles, and terminology.

## Current priorities

- [Atlantic Standard](atlantic-standard.md)
- Gulf Occidental
- Regional dialects and contact varieties
- Place-name standards
- Personal-name and title conventions
- Cross-language exonyms and endonyms

## Authority rule

The *AS Language Book* is authoritative for Atlantic Standard spelling, pronunciation, grammar, and naming questions. General political or historical claims found there should be checked against the broader HFR canon hierarchy.
