# Master Timeline

<span class="canon-badge status-hard">Canon chronological backbone</span>
<span class="canon-badge status-incomplete">Many exact mechanisms remain TBD</span>

This timeline records the main events that shape **How Few Remain** through its present year, **1604 ATL**. It prioritizes current project corrections, later/current material in *Rome (With Colonialism)* for intended direction, *Master Doc V3* for the organized chronology, and compatible detail from *Master Doc V2*.

## Status key

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **C** | Hard Canon |
| **S** | Soft Canon or approximate chronology |
| **TBD** | Known historical slot with unresolved mechanism or exact date |
| **R** | Retired or superseded present-day framework |

## Imperial Roman era

| Date | Status | Event | Historical consequence |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **9 ATL** | C | [Roman victory at Teutoburg](events/roman-victory-at-teutoburg.md) | Arminius is killed; Germania is subdued and integrated into the Roman system. |
| **165–180 ATL** | C | [Antonine Plague does not occur](events/no-antonine-plague.md) | Roman population, revenue, urban labor, and military recruitment remain stronger. |
| **c. 400 ATL** | C | [Rome reaches the Americas](events/roman-discovery-of-the-americas.md) | The Caribbean enters the Roman world; Atlantic colonization begins. |
| **c. 450–650 ATL** | C/S | First-wave Roman colonization | Coastal and river nodes develop in the Caribbean, East Coast, Gulf, and northern South America. Exact foundations are TBD. |
| **520 ATL** | C | [Christianity becomes the Roman state religion](events/christianization-of-rome.md) | Legal privilege for the Church and pressure on pagan institutions drive new migration patterns. |
| **520s–550s ATL** | C | Britain is lost from stable Roman control | Celtic and Anglo-Saxon polities prevail; the Isles begin their distinct composite history. |
| **600s ATL** | C/TBD | [Fall of the Western Roman Empire](events/fall-of-western-rome.md) | Western successor states emerge; American colonies become de facto independent; Byzantium survives. |

## Early medieval successor era, c. 600–1100

| Date | Status | Event | Historical consequence |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **c. 600–1000 ATL** | C | Francia, HRE, Byzantium, Novgorod, colonial successor states and other regional systems develop | Roman institutions persist in transformed political worlds. |
| **670 ATL** | C | Columbea and Nova Caeserea found the United States | Atlantic colonies create a federation for defense and common diplomacy. |
| **700 ATL** | C | Boralea joins the USA | Federation expands northward. |
| **750 ATL** | C | Chesapeakae joins the USA | Chesapeake region enters the federation. |
| **810 ATL** | C | Carolinea and Augustea join | A coastal road enables coordinated accession. |
| **c. 820 ATL** | C | Liga Caesarea forms | Texia, Mississippae and the Monterrey-coast remnant create an independent Gulf union. |
| **c. 840 ATL** | C | Appalachia joins the USA | A frontier dispute with Augustea ends in accession and territorial compromise. |
| **c. 860 ATL** | C | Floria joins after a major Seminole war | USA intervention extends the Atlantic federation into Floria. |
| **c. 1000 ATL** | C/TBD | Liga Caesarea is forced or coerced into the USA | Gulf resentment becomes a long-term foundation of later Legionary ideology; exact mechanism TBD. |
| **1000s ATL** | C | Novgorod adopts Orthodox Christianity | Byzantine influence becomes central to Novgorodian state identity. |
| **c. 1050 ATL** | S/C | Denmark Christianizes | Wider Scandinavian conversion follows in the 1100s. |
| **1100s ATL** | C | Gunpowder reaches Europe earlier than OTL | Military use expands gradually but does not immediately neutralize steppe operational power. |

| **1300s ATL** | C/S | Francia annexes England and Wales | Francian power expands across the western European and British worlds; exact chronology TBD. |
| **13th–14th centuries ATL** | C | Celtic Union forms | Ireland, Scotland and later North Umbria become a durable Celtic political system. |
| **1100–1400 ATL** | C | Second-wave age of exploration and colonial development | Old World states expand through ports, islands, concessions, and coastal systems; detailed ownership remains status-sensitive. |

## Early modern and industrial era, 1400–1600

| Date | Status | Event | Historical consequence |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **1450s ATL** | C | Novgorod stops paying tribute to the Golden Horde | Tributary survival turns into independent great-power expansion. |
| **1480s ATL** | C | Muscovy breaks free of the Horde | Competition for leadership of the Rus world intensifies. |
| **late 1400s–early 1500s ATL** | C/TBD | Novgorod–Muscovy wars | Two or three wars end in effective Muscovite annexation by a Novgorod-led state. Exact dates TBD. |
| **1500s ATL** | C | Industrial Revolution accelerates | Mechanization, modern firearms, rail systems, telegraphy and bureaucratic state power reshape war and society. |
| **1520s ATL** | C | [First Novgorod–Scandinavian war](events/novgorod-scandinavian-wars.md) | Novgorod takes part of Finland; Poland–Lithuania remains outside the war. |
| **1550s ATL** | C | [Baltic coalition war](events/novgorod-scandinavian-wars.md) | Poland–Lithuania joins Novgorod against Scandinavia. Novgorod gains the rest of Finland, Estonia, and northern/eastern Livonia; Poland–Lithuania gains Riga, Curonia, and southern Livonia. |
| **late 1550s onward** | C | Scandinavia centralizes after defeat | Denmark remains inside the state; unified command, taxation, conscription, and foreign policy expand. |
| **late 1550s onward** | C | Poland–Lithuanian victory reinforces decentralization | The commonwealth remains overly reliant on cavalry and negotiated mobilization entering the Great War. |
| **by 1550s–1604 ATL** | C/TBD | Hijez expansion absorbs Egypt and the former Abbasid polity | Current project correction supersedes older present-day Egypt–Abbasid independence. Exact conquest dates TBD. |
| **c. 1530s ATL** | S | USA reaches the Pacific and controls California | Continental expansion reaches its maximum before the Great Indian War. |
| **1566–1570 ATL** | C | [First Great Indian War](events/first-great-indian-war.md) | [USA](../polities/united-states.md) supremacy collapses; [MDE](../polities/mexica-double-eagle-empire.md), [UTA](../polities/united-tribes-of-america.md), [Iroquois](../polities/iroquois-confederacy.md), [NCR](../polities/new-california-republic.md), [New Vegas](../polities/new-vegas.md), and Basin polities define the new map. |
| **c. 1570 ATL onward** | C | [NCR](../polities/new-california-republic.md) and [New Vegas](../polities/new-vegas.md) emerge | California becomes independent; New Vegas becomes a neutral Mojave rail and trade hinge. |
| **1575–1581 ATL** | C | [Legionary Civil War](events/legionary-civil-war.md) | [Caesar's Legions](../polities/caesars-legions.md) secedes and survives despite a pyrrhic USA victory. |
| **late 1500s ATL** | C | [Franco-Roman War](events/franco-roman-war.md) | HRE humiliates Francia, gains Low Countries and former Danelaw territory, and becomes Europe's leading power. |
| **by c. 1600 ATL** | C | British partition hardens | HRE controls most former Danelaw lands; Francia retains western England and Wales; Celtic Union holds Ireland, Scotland and North Umbria. |

## Present era, 1600–1604 ATL

| Date | Status | Event | Historical consequence |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **c. 1600 ATL** | C | World reaches a broad OTL c. 1910 industrial baseline | Railways, telegraphy, machine guns, modern artillery, industrial fleets and mass bureaucracy dominate great-power competition. |
| **1600–1604 ATL** | C | Aviation remains behind the wider technology system | Aircraft exist mainly as rare prototypes rather than mature military or commercial systems. |
| **1604 ATL** | C | [Present-day world baseline](../world/world-in-1604.md) | Multipolar armed peace; exact Great War ignition chain and alliance blocs remain TBD. |

## Retired or superseded chronology

| Older framework | Current ruling |
|---|---|
| Egypt and Abbasids remain independent present-day rivals | **Superseded:** both are incorporated into the Hijez Sultanate by 1604 ATL. |
| Christianity becomes official in the 550s | **Superseded:** the locked date is 520 ATL. |
| Western Rome falls around 476 | **Non-canon:** western collapse occurs in the 600s ATL. |
| Novgorod takes all Finland only around one undifferentiated date | **Superseded:** part in the 1520s; remainder in the 1550s. |
| Denmark secedes and Novgorod takes all Baltic territories | **Superseded:** Denmark remains in centralized Scandinavia; Poland–Lithuania joins the 1550s war and receives Riga, Curonia, and southern Livonia. |
