# Wars & Events

<span class="canon-badge status-editorial">Historical backbone</span>

This section collects the major divergences, wars, migrations, and political crises that created the world of **1604 ATL**. Dates and outcomes follow the HFR source hierarchy. Where the sources preserve only a broad result, tactical details and treaty names remain **TBD** rather than being invented.

## Foundational Roman divergences

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

- **[Absence of the Antonine Plague](no-antonine-plague.md)** — preserves Roman population, revenue, manpower, and institutional capacity.
- **[Roman Victory at Teutoburg](roman-victory-at-teutoburg.md)** — brings Germania into the Roman system and reshapes western Europe.
- **[Roman Discovery and Colonization of the Americas](roman-discovery-of-the-americas.md)** — creates the Atlantic Roman world beginning around 400 ATL.
- **[Christianization of the Roman Empire](christianization-of-rome.md)** — makes Christianity official in 520 and drives the Christian East Coast / pagan Gulf split.
- **[Fall of the Western Roman Empire](fall-of-western-rome.md)** — fragments the stronger Roman world in the 600s and creates successor civilizations in both hemispheres.

</div>

## Medieval and early-modern transformations

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

- **[Mongol World-Shock and Ilkhanate Crisis](mongol-ilkhanate-crisis.md)** — remakes Eurasia, preserves a durable Horde, and creates the conditions for Byzantine recovery and Hijez consolidation.
- **[Novgorod–Scandinavian Wars](novgorod-scandinavian-wars.md)** — gives Novgorod Finland, Estonia, and northern/eastern Livonia; gives Poland–Lithuania Riga, Curonia, and southern Livonia; drives Scandinavian centralization.

</div>

## Industrial-era wars

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

- **[First Great Indian War](first-great-indian-war.md)** — expanded in v0.1 with the later-V3 campaign sequence and *Rome*-derived operational detail; breaks USA continental supremacy and establishes the modern multipolar Americas.
- **[Legionary Civil War](legionary-civil-war.md)** — divides the Atlantic Roman world between the USA and Caesar's Legions.
- **[Franco-Roman War](franco-roman-war.md)** — humiliates Francia, elevates the HRE, and partitions the British world.

</div>
