## Source conflicts currently resolved

### Hijez present-day correction

Egypt and the Abbasids no longer exist as independent states in 1604 ATL. Both are part of the Hijez Sultanate. Older V3 passages describing an Abbasid–Egyptian rivalry are superseded.

### Northern retcon

Denmark does not secede from Scandinavia. It remains within a centralized Scandinavian state in 1604. Poland–Lithuania joins Novgorod in the 1550s war and gains Riga, Curonia, and southern Livonia. Novgorod's Baltic holdings are now Finland, Estonia, and northern/eastern Livonia rather than every Baltic territory.

### Poland–Lithuania demographic correction

Poland–Lithuania has no Jewish communities. OTL demographic assumptions should not be imported into its article.
