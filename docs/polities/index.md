# Polities

Political entities use the [polity template](../templates/polity-template.md) and identify both their **article date** and **canon status**. Facts not supported by the current source hierarchy remain clearly marked **TBD**.

## Published articles

### New World

- [United States](united-states.md) — Atlantic Roman-successor federation; an industrial great power wounded by the Great Indian War and Legionary Civil War.
- [Caesar's Legions](caesars-legions.md) — pagan Gulf/interior Roman-successor state created by secession from the USA.
- [Mexica Double Eagle Empire](mexica-double-eagle-empire.md) — Mexica-led Mesoamerican industrial empire and Rio Grande great power.
- [United Tribes of America](united-tribes-of-america.md) — major plains and interior confederational power associated with cavalry and lever-action rifles.
- [Iroquois Confederacy](iroquois-confederacy.md) — northeastern great power of the Lake Ontario and St. Lawrence interior, allied with Vinland.
- [New California Republic](new-california-republic.md) — independent Californian successor state and western Pacific pole.
- [New Vegas](new-vegas.md) — neutral Mojave city-state, financial center, and rail hinge.
- [Vinland](vinland.md) — Scandinavian North Atlantic territorial power and ally of the Iroquois.

### Western and Central Europe

- [Holy Roman Empire](holy-roman-empire.md) — Europe's leading continental power and the dominant German-Roman imperial state.
- [Francia](francia.md) — former western hegemon, still a major Catholic and maritime power after defeat by the HRE.

### Northern and Eastern Europe

- [Scandinavia](scandinavia.md) — centralized northern maritime state uniting Denmark, Sweden, and Norway after its eastern defeats.
- [Novgorod](novgorod.md) — commercial and Orthodox Rus great power controlling Finland, Estonia, and northern/eastern Livonia.
- [Poland–Lithuania](poland-lithuania.md) — decentralized elective commonwealth controlling Riga, Curonia, and southern Livonia.
- [Golden Horde](golden-horde.md) — enduring non-Islamic steppe great power and principal southern rival of Novgorod.

### Eastern Mediterranean and Iranian world

- [Byzantium](byzantium.md) — surviving eastern Roman Empire controlling Constantinople, the Straits, most of Anatolia, and the southern Balkans.
- [Persia](persia.md) — enduring Iranian plateau great power linking the Near East, Central Asia, and India.
- [Hijez Sultanate](hijez-sultanate.md) — Islamic great power that incorporates Egypt and the former Abbasid state by 1604 ATL.
