## Overview

By **1604 ATL**, Egypt and the Abbasid polity no longer exist as independent states. Both have been absorbed into, or otherwise incorporated within, the **Hijez Sultanate**. This supersedes older material that treated Egypt and the Abbasids as independent rivals.

The Sultanate's exact constitutional structure, administrative divisions, capital, ruling dynasty, and territorial boundaries should be imported from the authoritative HFR sources rather than inferred here.
