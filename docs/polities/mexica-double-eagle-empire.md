## Overview

The **Mexica Double Eagle Empire**, abbreviated **MDE**, is the dominant imperial power of the Mexican and wider Mesoamerican world. It is one of North America's true industrial great powers and one of the clearest consequences of the setting's reduced Indigenous demographic collapse.

The MDE successfully resisted Old World recolonization and later became one of the principal enemies of the [United States](united-states.md). During the [First Great Indian War](../history/events/first-great-indian-war.md), its armies attacked through Sonora and Chihuahua, devastated USA forces in Texia and Monterrae, campaigned through the Mojave and Baja, and threatened the lower-Mississippi heartland. Its greatest advances were eventually checked, but the war permanently established the MDE as a continental power.

By 1604, the MDE holds the Rio Grande marches and extends into the New Mexico corridor. It no longer directly faces the USA; [Caesar's Legions](caesars-legions.md) now occupies the intervening frontier.

## Name and imperial identity

“Mexica Double Eagle Empire” is a working English-language project title. The final endonym, exonym, demonym, ruler's style, and formal meaning of the double-eagle symbol remain **TBD**.

The sources establish a **Mexica-led imperial polity** containing subordinate or absorbed Mesoamerican peoples. A possible dual structure uniting Mexica/Nahua and Maya political traditions has been proposed, with the double eagle expressing that union, but it is not locked.

Latin and Arabic influence exists in some elite, clerical, commercial, or court layers, but its scale and institutional role remain **TBD**.

## Territory and borders

### Core sphere

The MDE dominates the Mexican plateau and broader Mesoamerican world. Exact southern and coastal borders are not yet mapped.

### Northern marches

The hard-canon present-day frame establishes that the MDE:

- holds territory to the Rio Grande in the east;
- extends roughly one-third into OTL New Mexico along the river corridor;
- maintains northern marches through Sonora and Chihuahua;
- and directly faces Caesar's Legions rather than the USA.

The exact forts, river crossings, provincial boundaries, buffer peoples, and status of Baja remain **TBD**.

### Claims

Compatible V2 claims material records ambitions or claims toward:

- southern portions of NCR territory;
- Apache regions;
- parts of Navajo country;
- and influence toward New Vegas.

These claims should not be confused with established control.

## Government and administration

The MDE is an empire, but the following are unresolved:

- ruling dynasty or elective structure;
- title of the sovereign;
- relationship between Mexica, Maya, and other constituent peoples;
- provincial and tribute administration;
- military governorship of the northern marches;
- capital and secondary seats;
- legal pluralism;
- and the relationship between court, religious institutions, merchants, and regional elites.

The empire should not be reduced to a direct continuation of the OTL Aztec Triple Alliance. It has centuries of altered history, foreign contact, military modernization, and imperial development behind it.

## Society, culture, and religion

The MDE is a surviving and modernized Indigenous imperial civilization. It contains many Mesoamerican peoples under Mexica leadership, with social hierarchy and cultural diversity that have not yet been fully defined.

The exact religious system, degree of reform, treatment of conquered peoples, court language, regional languages, class structure, and incorporation of Latin or Arabic elements remain **TBD**.

The central interpretive rule is that industrialization has not erased Indigenous civilizational identity. The MDE is modern on its own historical foundations rather than modern because it became culturally European.

## Economy and infrastructure

The MDE is capable of sustaining industrial great-power warfare across deserts, mountains, coasts, and long continental supply routes. This implies substantial state capacity, logistics, arms production, taxation, and transport infrastructure.

Known strategic corridors include:

- the Rio Grande marches;
- Sonora and Chihuahua approaches;
- the Baja campaign route;
- Mojave and New Vegas approaches;
- and the Gulf route toward Texia and Monterrae.

Exact industrial centers, rail systems, ports, currency, population, and trade relationships remain **TBD**.

## Armed forces

The MDE demonstrated high operational capacity in the Great Indian War. Its forces:

- opened the southern front against the USA;
- destroyed or defeated major USA formations in Texia and Monterrae;
- campaigned through Sonora and the Mojave during summer;
- invaded Baja;
- drove toward the Gulf heartland;
- defeated a Californian detachment before besieging New Vegas;
- and remained capable of holding large postwar gains despite eventual battlefield reverses.

The exact military branches, command system, equipment doctrine, uniforms, unit names, and mobilization system remain **TBD**.

## First Great Indian War

The MDE and Iroquois opened the war with coordinated attacks intended to damage the USA's two principal industrial regions: the northeastern metropolitan belt and the lower-Mississippi/Gulf system.

The MDE's eastern army initially overwhelmed USA forces in the southwest and Gulf approaches. *Rome* develops the defense around the New Orleans-area heartland in much greater detail: the USA's southern commander, still called **Mr C**, musters state militias while outnumbered approximately two to one, conducts an apparent retreat near the Lafayette–Atchafalaya region, and draws the pursuing MDE army into a devastating ambush. V3 locks the commander, location type, and strategic reversal; the exact tactical sequence remains strong development material rather than fully finalized canon.

In the west, MDE forces defeat a Californian formation and besiege [New Vegas](new-vegas.md). The city survives through wealth, mercenaries, and surviving Californian support. The detailed mechanics of the siege remain **TBD**.

The war ends in exhaustion and stalemate. The MDE retreats from its deepest advances but retains the Rio Grande and New Mexico corridor, permanently breaking USA continental supremacy.

## Foreign relations

### Caesar's Legions

CL is now the MDE's immediate northern and northeastern rival. The precise armistice, inspection regime, and military frontier remain **TBD**.

### United States

The USA remains a historical enemy, but the states no longer share a direct frontier. Their diplomacy after the Legionary secession remains **TBD**.

### New California Republic and New Vegas

The MDE has frontier tensions and possible claims involving Baja, the southern NCR belt, Basin routes, and New Vegas approaches. Exact treaties and trade arrangements remain **TBD**.

### United Tribes of America and Iroquois Confederacy

All three fought against the USA in the Great Indian War, but a permanent alliance system has not been established. Their wartime coordination should not automatically be treated as a lasting bloc.
