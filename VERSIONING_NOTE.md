# Encyclopedia Versioning Note

Substantial encyclopedia additions continue as v0.x releases. Refinements to the same substantial update use patch versions such as v0.10.1, v0.10.2, and v0.10.3. The earlier development packages labeled v0.10.1, v0.10.2, and v0.10.3 correspond conceptually to v0.10.1, v0.10.2, and v0.10.3. The current package is v0.10.7. The project will not advance to v1.0 without explicit user approval.

- **v0.10.5** — visual cartography polish: black political borders everywhere and clearer selected-country emphasis; no geometry changes.

- **v0.10.6** — final globe cleanup: remove stray disconnected UTA fragments north of the Colorado system and add lightweight drag/zoom rendering for smoother motion.

- **v0.10.7** — major United States article expansion: federation history, Plains Mounted Confederation/Cahokia context, continental expansion, national summaries of the Great Indian War and Legionary Civil War, postwar centralization, former territories and claims, restorationism, economy, military, society, and foreign relations. Globe geometry is unchanged.
